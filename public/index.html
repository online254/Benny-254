HELP CENTRE

Frequently asked questions.

Does joining guarantee paid work?

No. Membership or account creation does not guarantee tasks, employment or earnings. Check each opportunity's requirements and terms.

How does M-Pesa activation work?

Select a package and enter your Kenyan phone number. If the backend accepts the request, follow the prompt on your phone. Payment is not confirmed until the transaction is verified.

Are the opportunity cards live jobs?

No. The cards on this version are illustrative category examples. Live opportunities should be published only when backed by verified platform records.

What should I check before paying?

Review the package price, exact benefits, terms, refund rules and support contact. Never share your M-Pesa PIN or one-time password.
