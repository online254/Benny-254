Help centre

Frequently asked questions.

Learn more about Online Sphere, digital opportunities, membership and account safety. If something is unclear, contact the official support team through the contact details published on our website.

What is Online Sphere?
Online Sphere is a digital platform designed to help people discover online opportunities, develop useful skills and explore participation in the digital economy.
How do I get started?
Explore the available pages, review each opportunity's requirements, and create an account through the official registration process when it is available.
What types of digital opportunities can I explore?
Categories may include transcription, data entry, writing, graphic design, video editing, translation, research and virtual assistance. Availability varies, so check the opportunities page for current listings.
What membership packages are listed?
The listed packages are Bronze at KSh 1,000, Silver at KSh 1,750 and Gold at KSh 2,500. Review the current membership terms and benefits before making a payment. A package does not guarantee work, earnings or employment.
How do M-Pesa payments work?
Where supported, you can request an M-Pesa payment prompt using your registered Kenyan phone number. Check the package and amount before approving the request on your phone. Only approve a payment you recognise.
What should I do if a payment fails?
Check your M-Pesa message and account balance first. Avoid making repeated payments until you confirm the status. Contact official support with the transaction details, but never share your M-Pesa PIN or account passwords.
Does Online Sphere guarantee earnings or jobs?
No. Opportunities, eligibility and payment depend on the specific work and its terms. Review all conditions carefully; never rely on guaranteed-income claims.
How do I keep my account safe?
Use your own accurate details, keep your password private, protect your phone and never disclose your M-Pesa PIN or verification codes to another person.
