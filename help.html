Getting started

What is Online Sphere?
Online Sphere is designed to help people discover digital work categories, explore learning resources and understand ways to participate in the digital economy.
How do I get started?
Begin by browsing the Opportunities and Learn pages. Read the information provided, assess what matches your skills and review applicable terms before registering or making a payment.
Does Online Sphere guarantee work or income?
No. Exploring the platform or purchasing a membership does not guarantee a job, a task, approval, earnings or a particular financial outcome. Opportunities depend on their actual availability, requirements and applicable terms.

Membership

What membership packages are listed?
The membership page currently lists Bronze at KSh 1,000, Silver at KSh 1,750 and Gold at KSh 2,500. Review the membership page for the displayed details and applicable terms before choosing a package.
Does paying for membership guarantee a payout?
No. Membership payment alone is not proof of a payout entitlement, employment or earnings. Read the package terms carefully and request clarification about any unclear fees or benefits before paying.
What should I check before purchasing?
Confirm the price, package benefits, eligibility rules, refund policy, renewal conditions and any additional charges. Only proceed when you understand the terms.

M-Pesa and payments

How does the M-Pesa STK Push work?
On the membership checkout, select a package and enter the M-Pesa phone number in the requested format. If the request is accepted, an STK prompt may appear on your phone. Check the amount and merchant details carefully before entering your M-Pesa PIN.
I tapped the payment button but nothing happened. What should I do?
Check your internet connection, make sure the phone number is correct, and try again only after checking whether a request is already pending. If the problem continues, note the error message and contact support through a verified official channel.
The STK prompt did not arrive. Was I charged?
A missing prompt does not by itself confirm whether a transaction occurred. Check your M-Pesa messages and transaction history. Do not immediately submit repeated payment requests. If money was deducted, retain the transaction confirmation and ask support to verify the payment status.
The website says the request was accepted. Is my payment complete?
Not necessarily. Acceptance of an STK request is different from confirmation of a successful payment. Wait for the official transaction result and verified payment status. Do not treat a loading message or request ID as proof of payment.
What information should I provide when reporting a payment issue?
Provide the package selected, approximate transaction time, phone number with sensitive digits masked, and the error message. If a transaction succeeded, share only the minimum transaction reference needed through a verified support channel. Never send your M-Pesa PIN, password or one-time security code.

Digital opportunities

What kinds of digital work can I explore?
Categories may include transcription, data entry, writing, graphic design, video editing, translation, research, virtual assistance and other digital services. A category listing is not confirmation that a live vacancy or paid task is available.
Are all listed categories currently accepting workers?
Not necessarily. Browse the Opportunities page and read each listing carefully. Availability, eligibility, deadlines and payment terms should be confirmed for the specific opportunity.
How can I prepare for online work?
Develop a relevant skill, practise with sample projects, prepare examples of your work and learn how to communicate professionally. Our learning page provides a starting point for exploring skills.

Account access

What if I cannot log in?
Check that you are using the correct login details and the official website address. If a password reset feature is available, follow its instructions. Never share your password or verification code with another person.
Why does my dashboard not show my information?
Confirm that you opened the correct page and that your internet connection is stable. A blank dashboard may indicate a page, script, authentication or server issue. Refresh once; if the problem persists, record the page address and visible error so the technical team can investigate.

Online safety

How can I protect myself from online scams?
Verify the organisation and opportunity independently. Be cautious of guaranteed returns, pressure to pay immediately, requests for PINs or passwords, and fees described vaguely. Keep receipts and read the terms before transferring money.
Should I pay an unexpected fee to release earnings?
Do not pay an unexpected fee without independently verifying its legitimacy, purpose and written terms. Be particularly cautious of requests for advance payments described as gas, unlocking, clearance or release fees. Never rely only on a screenshot or a message from an unverified account.
