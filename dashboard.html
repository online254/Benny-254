Help & Support

Information to help you use Online Sphere.

How M-Pesa payments work

1. Choose a membership package.

2. Enter the M-Pesa phone number registered for payment.

3. Tap Request M-Pesa Payment and follow the prompt on your phone.

4. Wait for the transaction to be verified by the payment provider and backend.

5. Keep your M-Pesa confirmation message for your records.

Important information

Never share your M-Pesa PIN or account password with anyone.

A payment prompt is not a successful payment confirmation.

Contact official Online Sphere support if a payment is deducted but not reflected in your account.
